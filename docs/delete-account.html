How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your SleepWell account, send an email to smartvoucherpk@gmail.com from the email address associated with your SleepWell account.

Use the subject line SleepWell account deletion. In your message, state that you want your SleepWell account deleted. Do not include your password.

If you cannot send the request from your account email, contact us and we may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account.

What will be deleted

  • Your SleepWell Firebase Authentication account, after we verify and process your request.
  • Any account information that SleepWell controls and is associated with that account.

Sleep preferences, routines, diary entries and progress in the current app version are stored locally on your device. They are not currently synchronised to SleepWell cloud storage. Remove this local information by clearing SleepWell app data or uninstalling the app.

What may be retained

Google Play may retain purchase, payment, tax or transaction records under its own policies and legal obligations. We may retain limited information where required by law or where necessary for security, fraud prevention or dispute resolution.

Processing time and help

We will review your request and may contact you if we need to verify account ownership. If you have questions about deletion, email smartvoucherpk@gmail.com.

For more information about how SleepWell handles information, read our Privacy Policy.
