SleepWell Privacy Policy

Better sleep. A calmer you.

Effective date: November 3, 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how SleepWell handles information when you use the SleepWell mobile application. SleepWell provides bedtime routines, breathing exercises, a sleep diary, progress features, reminders, ambient audio and optional Premium purchases.

Questions or privacy requests: smartvoucherpk@gmail.com.

2. Information the app handles

  • Account information: your email address and authentication information when you create an account or sign in using Firebase Authentication. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; SleepWell does not receive your plaintext password.
  • Sleep and app preferences: bedtime and wake-time preferences, sleep goals, routine steps, routine completion/progress, sleep diary ratings, energy/mood selections and notes you choose to enter.
  • Reminder settings: whether you enable bedtime reminders and the schedule needed to create local notifications on your device.
  • Purchase information: Google Play provides the app with purchase and restore status for Premium products. Payment card and billing details are processed by Google Play, not by SleepWell.
  • Technical information: Firebase or Google Play may process technical information needed to provide authentication, app distribution, billing and service security under their own terms and privacy documentation.

3. Where information is stored

Sleep preferences, routine information, diary entries and progress are currently stored locally on your device using app storage. They may be removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. SleepWell currently uses Firebase Authentication for account sign-in. The current app version does not provide cloud synchronisation of diary or routine entries.

Firebase Authentication processes account information using Google's infrastructure. Google Play processes purchases and subscription management. Ambient audio is streamed from the linked Wikimedia Commons media sources; when you play audio, your device connects to those sources and their operators may receive ordinary connection information.

4. How information is used

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To save and display your sleep preferences, routines, diary entries and progress on your device.
  • To schedule reminders you choose to enable.
  • To provide Premium purchase and restore flows through Google Play.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve the app and protect its services.

5. Sharing and third-party services

SleepWell does not sell your personal information. Information is processed by service providers only as needed for the functions described in this policy, including:

  • Google Firebase for account authentication and related infrastructure.
  • Google Play / Google for app distribution and in-app purchases.
  • Wikimedia Commons media hosting for ambient audio playback.

The current app version does not include third-party advertising or analytics functionality. If these services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local sleep data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall the app. You can request help with deletion of your SleepWell account information by emailing smartvoucherpk@gmail.com from your account email and writing “SleepWell account deletion” in the subject. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request. Some records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

7. Children’s privacy

SleepWell is not designed specifically for children under 13, and we do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided account information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Security

We use the security features provided by our service providers and the Android platform. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
